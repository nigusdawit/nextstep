Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need for my consultation?
For the initial free consultation, just bring yourself and a general idea of your income sources. For filing, we will provide a secure checklist of required documents (W-2s, 1099s, etc.).
Do you work with out-of-state clients?
Yes! While we are certified in Maryland, we have the technology and expertise to assist clients remotely across various states.
How are wealth planning and taxes connected?
Taxes are often your biggest annual expense. By reducing your tax liability through legal strategies, you free up more capital to invest, save, and grow your wealth.
